# EP02 Design Notes

## Overview

Episode 02 is the first phase where:

- Structure
- Constraints
- Narrative traps

are designed as a unified system.

---

## Core Objective

> Design misdirection as structure, not as trick.

---

## Key Elements

### 1. Scene Card System (SC)

- Narrative is divided into SC units
- Each SC has a defined role
- Structure is fixed and governed

---

### 2. Trap Design

EP02 introduces:

- Intentional misinterpretation
- Explicit “false assumptions”
- Controlled reader perception

---

## Trap Concept (SC01)

### Situation

- Abandoned school building (north wing)
- Underground rainwater tank
- Horror house setting

---

### Setup

- Mizuha hides in a locker
- Tank is normally empty
- Students walk through the space

---

### Mao’s Plan

- Slightly open water valve
- Water rises slowly
- No real danger intended
- Create urgency through false warning

---

### Narrative Lie

> “If we don’t stop it, she will drown.”

Initially false.

---

### Unexpected Event

- Valve failure
- Water flow increases
- Locker cannot open due to pressure
- Real danger emerges

---

### Outcome

- Situation becomes life-threatening
- Shunta does NOT use magic
- Physical rescue

---

## Structural Role

This trap:

- Aligns reader perception with Mao
- Creates false assumptions
- Breaks them simultaneously

---

## Design Significance

EP02 demonstrates:

- Narrative as constraint system
- Controlled misdirection
- Failure of incorrect assumptions

---

## Current Status

- Structure defined
- Trap validated
- Used as testbed for governance rules

---
